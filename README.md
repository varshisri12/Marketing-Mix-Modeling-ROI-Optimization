# Marketing Mix Modeling & ROI Optimization

A regression-based Marketing Mix Modeling (MMM) project designed to understand how different marketing channels influence sales, estimate channel-level ROI, and identify an efficient way to allocate a fixed marketing budget.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

Companies invest in multiple marketing channels such as TV advertising, digital advertising, and social media. However, simply knowing how much money was spent on each channel does not tell us which channels are actually contributing to sales.

This project uses historical weekly marketing data to:

- Analyze sales and marketing trends
- Measure the impact of different marketing channels on sales
- Account for advertising carryover effects using Adstock
- Build a regression-based Marketing Mix Model
- Estimate incremental revenue contribution
- Calculate ROI for each marketing channel
- Optimize the marketing budget across channels
- Generate data-driven business recommendations

The project demonstrates how data analytics and statistical modeling can be used to support marketing and business decisions.

---

## 🎯 Business Problem

A company is spending money across different marketing channels, but management wants to answer questions such as:

1. Which marketing channel contributes the most to sales?
2. How much incremental revenue is associated with each channel?
3. Which channel provides the best estimated ROI?
4. Does advertising continue to influence sales after the initial campaign?
5. How should a fixed marketing budget be distributed across channels?
6. How can marketing decisions be made using data instead of assumptions?

This project attempts to answer these questions using Marketing Mix Modeling.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

The main objectives of this project are:

- Understand the relationship between marketing spend and sales
- Analyze the performance of TV, Digital, and Social Media channels
- Apply Adstock transformation to capture advertising carryover effects
- Build a regression-based Marketing Mix Model
- Estimate the contribution of individual marketing channels
- Calculate channel-level ROI
- Optimize marketing budget allocation
- Create visualizations for business interpretation
- Convert statistical results into actionable business recommendations
